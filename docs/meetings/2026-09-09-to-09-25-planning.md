## Decisions and questions carried forward

- The application targets one-person/small-household cooking with limited ingredients and tools.
- The team wanted photo-assisted entry to reduce typing, while keeping user review because detection can be wrong.
- The complete inventory model, recommendation ranking, and AI adaptation rules required later technical work.

The first and final proposal files are managed as course submission sources outside this repository. See [Iteration 1 status](../iterations/iteration-1-status.md) for implementation evidence.
